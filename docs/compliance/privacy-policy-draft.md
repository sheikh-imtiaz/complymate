# Privacy policy (draft — for legal review)

ComplyMate Pty Ltd ("we") provides compliance software to accounting, legal, real estate and trust and company
service firms ("customers"). This draft follows the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

## Roles
* For **customer user accounts** we are the controller of account data (name, email, sign-in and security events).
* For **customers' client data** (identity details, documents, screening, risk and reports) we act on the
  customer's instructions as a service provider. Customers are responsible for collection notices to their clients.

## What we collect
Account details; firm details; client records and documents that customers upload; usage and security logs;
billing details (processed by Stripe — we do not store card numbers).

## How we use it
To provide the service (screening, risk scoring, program building, reporting), secure accounts, support customers
(with consent and time limits), bill, and improve the service using aggregated, de-identified metrics.
We do not sell personal information. We do not use customer client data to train AI models.

## AI services
Questions and rule-library extracts are processed by AI models hosted in Australia (Amazon Bedrock, Sydney region,
or Azure Australia East). Client data reaches the model only when a permitted user's question requires it.

## Storage, security and retention
Data is stored in Australia (or New Zealand/Australia for NZ customers by choice), encrypted in transit and at rest,
with MFA, role-based access and an immutable audit log. AML/CTF records are kept for at least 7 years as required
by law, then deleted or de-identified on the customer's instruction.

## Overseas disclosure
None by default. Sub-processors: AWS (or Microsoft Azure), Stripe (payments), email delivery in-region.

## Access and correction, complaints
Contact privacy@complymate.example. If unresolved, contact the OAIC (AU) or the Privacy Commissioner (NZ).

## Data breaches
We notify affected customers and regulators under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme / Privacy Act 2020 Part 6.
